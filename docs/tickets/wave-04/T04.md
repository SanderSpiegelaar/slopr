## Goal

A developer can silence one finding or one file, but only with a written reason.

## Scope

- `suppress.rs`: parse directives per Inline suppressions, from `SourceFile::comments()` for parsed files and from raw lines for text files. Filter findings, and emit `suppress/invalid-directive` findings.
- `main.rs`: apply suppressions after rules (and later after fallow), before the gate.
- `report/sarif.rs`: list `suppress/invalid-directive` in the driver rules.
- Fixtures in `tests/fixtures/suppress/`, using `ts/unsafe-assertion` as the rule.
- Tests: `suppress_next_line`, `suppress_next_line_skips_blank`, `suppress_file`, `suppress_multiple_ids`, `suppress_missing_reason_is_invalid`, `suppress_unknown_rule_is_invalid`, `suppress_fallow_prefix_accepted`, `suppress_invalid_cannot_be_suppressed`, `suppress_block_comment_targets_line_after_comment`, `suppress_partly_invalid_suppresses_nothing`, `suppress_mention_mid_line_ignored`.

## Out of scope

- Baseline, which is V02.

## Acceptance criteria

- The eleven tests pass.
- A suppressed finding is absent from all three formats.
