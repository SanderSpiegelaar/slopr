## Goal

A user who sees a finding can learn what the rule catches and where it came from.

## Scope

- `build.rs` per Explain data, with `cargo:rerun-if-changed` on the fixtures dir and `rules.md`.
- `slopr explain <RULE>` prints id, default severity, on or off, summary, the Sources cell, then the bad example. An unknown rule exits 2.
- Tests: `explain_known_rule` (snapshot), `explain_unknown_rule_exits_2`.

## Out of scope

- Explaining `fallow/*` rules.

## Acceptance criteria
